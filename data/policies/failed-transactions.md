## Resolution timelines

For a failed UPI or IMPS transfer where the customer's account was debited but the beneficiary was not credited, the applicable resolution timeline is T+1 day, where T is the transaction date. For an ATM cash withdrawal where the account was debited but cash was not dispensed, the applicable timeline is T+5 days.

## Compensation for delay

If the amount is not returned or the transaction is not resolved within the applicable timeline, compensation of ₹100 per day is payable for each day of delay after that timeline.

## Raising a complaint

Raise a complaint with the bank and provide the transaction's UTR (Unique Transaction Reference), transaction date, amount and channel. Keep the complaint reference for follow-up.

## Escalation to the Ombudsman

If the bank has not resolved the complaint within 30 days, or the response is unsatisfactory, the customer may escalate the complaint to the RBI Ombudsman.
