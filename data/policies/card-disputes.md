## Unrecognised card transaction

Report any card transaction you do not recognise to the bank immediately. Share the transaction date, amount and merchant so the bank can identify it and register a dispute.

## Blocking a card

Block the affected card immediately through the bank's authorised channel to prevent further transactions. Ask the bank to confirm that the card is blocked and request a replacement if needed.

## Zero liability reporting window

The customer has zero liability for an unauthorised transaction when it is reported within 3 working days of receiving the bank's transaction communication. Report it as soon as possible and retain the complaint reference.
